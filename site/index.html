How a verdict is reached

The method in full
01

Registry card intake

We take the agent's card exactly as a public registry lists it. The card's claims are the scope of the check.

02

Claims back-check

Each claim is checked against what the agent actually serves. Results are graded claim by claim.

03

Human testing validation

Human testers exercise the claims a machine cannot settle. Their test record becomes the evidence.
