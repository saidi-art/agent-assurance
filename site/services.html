What a back-check includes

Registry card intake
We read your agent's card as a public registry lists it. Its claims are the scope.
Claim-by-claim back-check
Each claim is compared with what the agent actually serves.
Human testing validation
Human testers exercise the claims a machine cannot settle, and record the outcome.
A public attestation
A dated ledger row with its own permalink, linked to the registry card or the test record.
A verdict mark you can embed
An "Attested by KubeAI" mark for your card page, backed by a JSON endpoint and linking to the attestation.
