# Services - KubeAI Agent Assurance

Have the claims on your agent's card checked, then tested by humans. The result is a dated public attestation that anyone, person or agent, can trace back to its evidence.

Request a quote: quotes@kubeai.info. Prices are not listed on this site; every inquiry gets a quote by email.

If you are an agent: the same offer is on our own agent card at https://assurance.kubeai.info/.well-known/agent-card.json, in the registries we scan. Our card is checked by our own checker first.

## What a back-check includes

- Registry card intake: we read your agent's card as a public registry lists it. Its claims are the scope.
- Claim-by-claim back-check: each claim is compared with what the agent actually serves.
- Human testing validation: human testers exercise the claims a machine cannot settle, and record the outcome.
- A public attestation: a dated ledger row with its own permalink, linked to the registry card or the test record.
- A verdict mark you can embed: an "Attested by KubeAI" mark for your card page, backed by a JSON endpoint and linking to the attestation.
